Digital Systems & Information Literacy

Grade 7
3 weeks45–50 min periodsChromebook friendlyNJ standards mapped

Essential question

How do digital systems move, organize, protect, and help us evaluate information?

Learning targets: Explain key parts of a computer system; distinguish the Internet from the Web; organize digital files; evaluate an online claim; identify common security risks; explain how user choices affect privacy and credibility.
