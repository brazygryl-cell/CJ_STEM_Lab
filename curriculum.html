Grades 6–8 core pathway

A coherent progression from inquiry to digital fluency to computer science.

CJ STEM Lab is designed so the middle-school experience connects across grade levels instead of feeling like three unrelated technology electives. Each course combines explicit instruction, guided practice, authentic application, revision, and evidence of learning.

Instructional model

Teach it clearly. Practice it together. Apply it meaningfully.

Project-based work is anchored in explicit teaching so students receive the concepts and examples they need before they are asked to design, investigate, or code independently.

1. Explicit instruction

Vocabulary, concepts, examples, and modeling.

2. Guided practice

Discussion, worked examples, and teacher-supported attempts.

3. Application

Investigation, design challenge, code, data task, or real problem.

4. Test & revise

Debug, improve, compare evidence, and make changes.

5. Explain & reflect

Show what changed, why it changed, and what was learned.

Standards strategy

Built around New Jersey requirements, with room to travel.

The first commercial pathway is being mapped to applicable New Jersey Student Learning Standards for Computer Science and Design Thinking, Career Readiness, Life Literacies and Key Skills, and selected Science connections. National frameworks can support internal mapping and future portability, but CJ STEM Lab will not describe CSTA alignment as formally validated unless an external review supports that claim.
