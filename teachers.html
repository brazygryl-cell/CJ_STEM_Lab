For classroom teachers

A coherent course you can actually teach tomorrow.

CJ STEM Lab is designed around the work teachers normally have to build themselves: pacing, explanations, examples, slides, student practice, projects, assessments, reteaching, and schedule changes.

Inside a complete lesson

One sequence from bell ringer to evidence of learning.

The goal is to reduce the “now I have to make the rest of it” problem. A lesson should arrive with the instructional pieces needed to teach, assess, adapt, and close it.

Daily lesson plans

Learning objective, applicable standards, vocabulary, materials, preparation, timing, bell ringer, direct instruction, guided practice, application, checks for understanding, closure, and extension.

Instructional slides

Clear visual instruction that introduces concepts, models examples, supports discussion, and gives students enough background to work confidently.

Student materials

Student notes, investigations, worksheets, coding tasks, design briefs, data tables, reflection prompts, and project documentation.

Assessments

Formative checks, performance tasks, rubrics, revision opportunities, and summative evidence connected to the lesson target.

Answer materials

Answer keys, worked examples, exemplars, anticipated errors, misconceptions, and teacher-facing guidance for open-ended work.

Differentiation

Scaffolds for multilingual learners and students needing additional support, accessibility considerations, low-tech alternatives, reteaching, and enrichment.

Schedule flexibility

The same course should survive the calendar.

Schools do not all run the same bell schedule, and even one classroom can lose time to shortened days, assemblies, testing, trips, and interruptions. Schedule options are part of the product—not an afterthought.

Full-year

Complete progression with projects, revision cycles, and capstone time.

Semester / quarter

Priority units and condensed pacing for shorter course windows.

45-minute periods

Lesson components sized for realistic middle-school class periods.

Rotation / enrichment

Modular units for specials, clubs, intervention, or enrichment blocks.

Content confidence

Support for teachers with different computer science backgrounds.

Not every middle-school technology teacher enters the classroom through the same certification route or with the same programming experience. Teacher-facing notes explain the content, anticipate misconceptions, show worked examples, and give facilitation prompts so the curriculum does not assume expert knowledge.

What stays teacher-led

Judgment matters.

Automated practice can support factual recall and constrained exercises later, but design quality, reasoning, originality, communication, and revision still need teacher judgment. CJ STEM Lab is designed to support the teacher, not reduce the course to time-on-screen.
